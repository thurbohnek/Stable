Delete your data – Stable

Stable by Studio.grane (Ørjan Grane, Steinbakken 93, 8610 Mo i Rana, Norway) · studio@grane.app

In short: Stable has no accounts. If you used the online features, you can delete everything we store about you yourself, right in the game. It happens at once.

Option 1: in the game (fastest)

  1. Open Stable and tap Settings.
  2. Tap Backup and restore code.
  3. Tap Delete my online data, then tap it again to confirm.

Your data is deleted from our server straight away, and online features are turned off on that phone.

Option 2: by email

If you no longer have the game, email studio@grane.app with the subject Delete my Stable data. Include your restore code (Settings → Backup and restore code) or, if you do not have it, the player name you used on the leaderboards. Because we do not know who you are, this helps us find the right data. We delete it within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • your random player ID;
  • your player name and all your scores on every leaderboard (Endless, weekly tournament, Daily challenge, Rhythm, Wobble and Level);
  • your cloud save and restore code;
  • feedback and name reports you have sent.

What is kept

Nothing that is linked to you. Our database provider may keep encrypted backups for a short time; deleted data disappears from those within 30 days.

Your game progress on your own phone stays there until you use Reset progress in Hall of Fame or uninstall the app.

Even if you never delete anything, online data that has not been used for 24 months and feedback older than 12 months are deleted automatically.
