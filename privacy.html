Privacy Policy – Stable

Last updated: October 9, 2026 · Applies to the Stable app on Google Play, the iPhone home-screen version and studio.grane.app/stable

In short: Stable needs no account and never asks for your real name, email or phone number. We do not show ads, do not track you and do not sell or share your data. If you use the online features (leaderboards, weekly tournament and cloud save), we store a random player ID, the nickname you pick, your scores and a backup of your game. You can turn online features off and delete your online data at any time in Settings.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Ørjan Grane, trading as Studio.grane, Steinbakken 93, Norway. Contact: studio.grane@gmail.com. We are a very small developer and do not have a data protection officer; write to the address above about anything in this policy.

3. Who processes data for us

  • Supabase (Supabase Inc.): database and anonymous login. Our data is stored on servers in the EU (Stockholm, Sweden).
  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare Inc.): hosting and delivery of studio.grane.app.
  • GitHub (GitHub Inc.): hosting of the older game address thurbohnek.github.io/Stable.
  • Google: if you install Stable from Google Play, Google handles the download (and later any purchases) under its own privacy policy.

These providers act on our instructions under data processing agreements. Some are US companies or have support staff outside the EU/EEA; such transfers are protected by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and/or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

4. Children

Stable is made for all ages. We never ask for real names, contact details or age, and we advise players to use a nickname, not their full name. Offensive names are blocked, and every name on the leaderboards can be reported. Parents can turn online features off in Settings, delete the online data with Settings → Backup and restore code → Delete my online data, or email us and we delete it for them.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your data and get a copy (portability): email us your restore code (Settings → Backup and restore code);
  • correct it: change your nickname in Hall of Fame;
  • delete it: Delete my online data in the game, or email us;
  • object and restrict processing: turn Online features off in Settings;
  • complain to a data protection authority. In Norway this is Datatilsynet; in other EU/EEA countries, your local authority.

Because we do not know who you are, we may ask for your restore code so we find the right data. We answer within one month.

6. Cookies and local storage

Stable uses no cookies. The game saves your progress, settings and (with online features on) its login token in your device's local storage. This is strictly necessary for the game to work, so no consent banner is needed. studio.grane.app uses no cookies, no analytics and no external fonts.

7. Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Players can only read and change their own cloud save; everything that ends up on the leaderboards is checked by the server.

8. Changes

If we change how we handle data, for example if ads or purchases are added, we update this page before the change and say so in the game's What's new.
