Privacy Policy – Stable

Developer: Studio.grane
Last updated: October 9, 2026

No account needed

Stable does not ask for your name, email, phone number or any login. We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress (scores, coins, unlocked items and settings) is saved on your own device.

What is stored online

When you are online, the game creates an anonymous player ID (a random number, not linked to who you are) and uses it to store:

  • Leaderboards: the player name you choose in the game, your best Endless score and your weekly tournament score. These are shown to other players.
  • Cloud save: a backup of your game progress and a restore code, so you can move your progress to a new phone. Only someone with your code can restore it.
  • Feedback: if you use "Send feedback", the text you write plus basic technical details (game mode, level, screen size, device type and language) so we can fix problems.

This data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, on servers in the EU (Stockholm, Sweden). It is only used to run these game features.

Advertising

The current version contains no third-party ads. If ads are added in a future update, this policy will be updated to describe the ad provider and the data it uses.

Children

Stable does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Please do not use your real full name as your player name.

Deleting your data

"Reset progress" in the game removes your progress from your device and your scores from the leaderboards. To have your online data (cloud save, scores and feedback) deleted completely, email us and include your restore code from Settings, Cloud save. We delete it within 30 days.
