Rebirth ♻️

×1 → ×2

Start over and multiply everything you earn: coins, sunflowers and XP. There are 10 rebirths, and each one is a big step: ×2, ×3, ×5, ×8 … all the way to ×100. Every rebirth unlocks something new (titles, biomes, blocks or pets), and the first three widen the perfect window by 1 pixel each. You also get a 🌻 sunflower gift to start each new rebirth. Skilled players earn a skill bonus of up to ×2 coins.

What happens

🔄 Reset: coins, sunflowers, player level, campaign stages and power-ups.
✅ Kept: blocks, pets, town, biomes, achievements and records.
💰 You keep 25% of the coins you have above the rebirth cost, and all coins and sunflowers you bought in the shop.
